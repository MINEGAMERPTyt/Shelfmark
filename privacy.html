Privacy

Last updated 9 September 2026

Overview

Shelfmark is a personal collection archive. This privacy notice explains what information is stored when you create and use a Shelfmark account.

Information Shelfmark stores

When you use Shelfmark, the application may store:

  • Your email address and authentication information required to operate your account.
  • Profile information you choose to provide.
  • Collection information such as titles, platforms, condition, purchase information, estimated values and notes.
  • Images you choose to upload for items in your collection.
  • Technical information required by the hosting and backend services used to operate Shelfmark.

How the information is used

Information stored by Shelfmark is used to provide your account, maintain your personal collection archive, display your saved items and images, and provide the features of the application.

Shelfmark does not sell personal information and does not use collection information for advertising.

Service providers

Shelfmark currently relies on third-party infrastructure to operate the application.

Supabase
Provides authentication, PostgreSQL database services and private image storage.
Netlify
Hosts and delivers the Shelfmark web application.

These providers may process technical information necessary to deliver their services according to their own privacy and security policies.

Authentication and browser storage

Shelfmark uses browser storage where necessary to maintain an authenticated Supabase session. This allows you to remain signed in between page loads.

Market research links

Shelfmark may provide links to external services such as PriceCharting, eBay and CeX to help users research item values.

Shelfmark does not automatically retrieve or store pricing information from those services. When you choose to open an external link, you leave Shelfmark and the privacy policy of that external service applies.

Data retention

Your active Shelfmark collection data is retained while your account exists or until you remove individual items.

When you permanently delete your account, Shelfmark removes the account's collection records and uploaded Shelfmark images as part of the deletion process. Infrastructure providers may retain limited backup or operational records according to their own retention policies.

Your choices and rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or objection regarding your personal information.

Shelfmark also provides an account deletion option for permanently removing your account and associated collection data.
